ProfileSeg v3.1 — independent real-data validation: protocol

Written 2026-10-27, before any read of this dataset was downloaded or inspected. Method frozen at the v3.1 code on branch profileseg-v3 (regional.py as of commit c3e17ec). Results will be added in a separate section below; changes to this protocol after data are seen will be listed as deviations.

Dataset

GEO GSE346229 (BioProject PRJNA1523976; released 2026; not used at any point during development; ATAC-seq, an assay type not used during development). S. cerevisiae, paired-end, NextSeq 2000.

  • Primary: Sth1 anchor-away (RSC catalytic subunit depletion), 0 h (rep1–4) versus 1 h (rep1–4).
  • Secondary: Snf2 anchor-away (SWI/SNF), 0 h (rep1–3) versus 1 h (rep1–3).

Prior biological expectation (literature, not these data): RSC depletion narrows nucleosome-depleted regions — the −1 and +1 nucleosomes move into the NDR — and reduces NDR accessibility genome-wide (Kubik et al. 2018 and others). SWI/SNF depletion has more restricted effects.

Processing (fixed)

bowtie2 --very-sensitive -X 1000 to sacCer3; MAPQ ≥ 10, proper pairs, duplicates removed (samtools markdup); Tn5 offset (+4 / −5). Fragment classes: sub-nucleosomal (≤ 120 bp; NDR accessibility) and nucleosomal (140–200 bp; nucleosome positions). Signal = fragment midpoints (ProfileSeg BAM path, midpoint mode), 10 bp bins.

Anchors (condition-blind)

Gene starts (ATG) from the sacCer3 RefSeq annotation, nuclear chromosomes, genes ≥ 500 bp apart from the nearest other gene start on the same strand. From the nucleosomal-fragment midpoints of all eight Sth1 samples pooled with equal weight (labels not used):

  • +1 nucleosome dyad: maximum of the smoothed pooled density in [ATG − 200, ATG + 100] (oriented);
  • −1 nucleosome dyad: maximum in [+1 − 450, +1 − 200];
  • NDR centre: midpoint between −1 and +1.

Windows: ±300 bp around each anchor (oriented by gene strand). Analyses per anchor set: +1 and −1 with nucleosomal fragments; NDR centre with sub-nucleosomal fragments.

Analyses and criteria

ProfileSeg v3.1 defaults (shape_any, shape_kernel, shift; BH 5 %). diffSplice comparator fixed as in round 6: edgeR QL diffSpliceDGE, support bins of the background-corrected pooled profile merged to 60 bp, gene-level F.

A. Real-null splits. Within-condition 2 vs 2 splits: 3 within Sth1 0 h, 3 within Sth1 1 h (6 splits), for each anchor set. Criteria (per test, per anchor set): - A1: mean share of regions called over the 6 splits ≤ 0.005 for shape_any; - A2: no single split with more than 1 % of regions called by shape_any. Reported: per-split calls for all tests and diffSplice, and the label-free-of-p-values replicate-pairing ratio.

B. Fragment-level insertion on the null splits. In each of the 6 splits, 20 % of regions (random, fixed seed) are modified in the second group only, at the level of individual fragments before binning: translation of nucleosomal-fragment midpoints by 20 or 40 bp (random sign per region); broadening (each midpoint jittered by N(0, 30 bp)); amplitude-only (binomial thinning of fragments by 0.5, which keeps the shape). Two insertion sets per split. Criteria for shape_any: - B1: mean FDP over runs ≤ 0.075 for each translation and broadening insertion; - B2: amplitude-only: mean share of regions called ≤ 0.01. Reported, not judged: power of all tests; end-to-end power and FDP of diffSplice on the same counts; ProfileSeg translation estimate ÷ true and coverage for consistent calls.

C. Biology (Sth1 1 h vs 0 h, 4 vs 4; Snf2 secondary). Predictions: - C1: at +1 nucleosomes, among shift calls with a consistent translation, the majority move upstream (towards the NDR; negative in the oriented frame): share > 0.5, one-sided binomial p < 0.05; - C2: at −1 nucleosomes, the majority of consistent calls move downstream (towards the NDR): share > 0.5, binomial p < 0.05; - C3: Sth1 has more shape_any calls than Snf2 at +1 and −1 nucleosomes (share of regions; Snf2 is 3 vs 3, so Sth1 is also run on every 3 vs 3 subset of its replicates and the comparison uses the mean). Reported descriptively: sub-nucleosomal NDR signal change (amplitude tests are not claimed), diffSplice calls and overlap with ProfileSeg, replicate-structure diagnostics, depth.

A failure of any criterion is reported as such; the method is not changed in response to these data.

Amendment before data (2026-10-27, before download)

The ProfileSeg BAM reader does not apply the Tn5 +4 / −5 offset. It moves fragment midpoints by 0.5 bp (negligible at 10 bp bins) and lengths by 9 bp, so the offset is not applied and the size classes are defined on the unshifted TLEN as written above (≤ 120 bp; 140–200 bp). Fragment-level insertion needs per-fragment midpoints, so midpoints are extracted once per sample with the same filters as the BAM reader (MAPQ ≥ 10, proper pairs, duplicates removed, each fragment counted once from its leftmost mate) and binned per experiment; the unmodified counts are cross-checked against the ProfileSeg BAM path for one sample. ProfileSeg configuration for every anchor set (fixed before data, by analogy with the earlier yeast settings): flank 600 bp, bins 10 bp, core ±300 bp (the analysed window), background = mean of the outer 200 bp on each side, oriented by gene strand. Fragments are extracted ±1300 bp around each gene start so that insertions of up to 40 bp move fragments into and out of the window correctly.

Results (2026-10-27; added after the run)

Run: commit c3e17ec, no uncommitted source changes, regional.py 87f0c6c86498c6a5; protocol file as amended before download (sha256 a5234324972ab7af; the first version, before the two amendments, was 5f2f19efe1626ff3). Outputs: benchmarks/results/independent_gse346229/ (INDEPENDENT_VALIDATION.md, JSON, alignment summary). Processing: 14 samples, alignment 87–96 %, 0.78–2.78 M deduplicated fragments per sample. The extracted fragment counts were identical, bin by bin, to the ProfileSeg BAM path for the checked sample (400 regions).

criterion result
A1 / A2 real-null splits (shape_any share ≤ 0.005; no split > 1 %) pass for all three anchor sets: no ProfileSeg call in any of the 18 null analyses (diffSplice: 0–0.07 % of regions)
B1 insertion FDP ≤ 0.075 pass (shape_any mean FDP 0.000–0.055; the tolerance, not the nominal 0.05)
B2 amplitude-only calls ≤ 1 % pass (0 calls)
C1 / C2 direction at +1 / −1 nucleosomes fail — not evaluable: no region was called by any method, ProfileSeg or diffSplice
C3 Sth1 more calls than Snf2 fail (0 vs 0)

Power was low throughout: translations of 20 / 40 bp inserted at the fragment level were detected in 4–12 % of regions by shape_any (shift 8–15 %; diffSplice 0–9 %), broadening in 0 %. The reason was flagged by the depth diagnostic in every analysis: a median of 18 (+1), 24 (−1) and 44 (NDR, sub-nucleosomal) fragments per region and sample in the ±300 bp windows, below the ~50 at which per-region shape tests have power, and only about half of the regions testable. Translation estimates for the few consistent calls were accurate (direction 1.00, estimate ÷ true 0.96–1.06; coverage 0.08–0.15).

What this validation shows, and what it does not:

  • It supports, on an independent dataset of a new assay type, that the shape tests do not produce false calls on within-condition splits or under amplitude-only changes, and that FDP for inserted changes stays within the pre-specified tolerance (≤ 0.075; point estimates 0.000–0.055, i.e. one cell slightly above the nominal 0.05). With 12 runs per cell the FDP estimates are imprecise; neither a single high run nor these means establish or refute FDR control.
  • It does not provide positive biological evidence: at this depth no method detected per-region changes. The protocol did not check depth before selecting the dataset, which in hindsight should have been a selection criterion.

Exploratory, not pre-specified: aggregate replicate-level analysis

To ask whether the expected biology is present at all, per-sample meta-profiles over all regions were compared between 1 h and 0 h with replicates as units (AGGREGATE_EXPLORATORY.md). Sth1 depletion moved the +1 dyad by −1.9 bp (towards the NDR; Welch p = 0.07) and the −1 dyad by +1.0 bp (towards the NDR; p = 0.12); NDR accessibility and the −1 to +1 distance did not change detectably. The direction matches the literature, but the genome-wide effect at 1 h in these data is about 2 bp — far below what any per-region test can resolve at this depth. The prior expectation written in the protocol (clear NDR narrowing) was too strong for this time point.

Consequences

  1. The calibration claims gain an independent, new-assay replication; the power and biology claims do not.
  2. A depth requirement belongs in dataset selection for positive validation (e.g. a median of ≥ 100 fragments per region and sample in the analysed window, estimable from total depth before analysis).
  3. A positive biological validation still needs a deeper dataset with a larger expected per-region effect.
